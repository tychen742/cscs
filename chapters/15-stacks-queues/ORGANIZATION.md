# Chapter Organization — Stacks, Queues, and Deques

## Learning Objectives

Students should be able to specify stack, queue, and deque operations, choose
an implementation, and apply these structures to practical problems.

## Sequence

- Stack interface and LIFO behavior
- Queue interface and FIFO behavior
- Deques and restricted operations
- Array-based and linked implementations
- Application lab and testing
