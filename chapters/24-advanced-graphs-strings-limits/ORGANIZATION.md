# Chapter Organization — Advanced Graphs, Strings, and Limits

## Learning Objectives

Students should be able to implement graph traversal, explain shortest-path
assumptions, compare string-search strategies, and recognize when a problem's
computational growth makes exact solutions impractical.

## Sequence

- Breadth-first and depth-first traversal
- Shortest paths and path reconstruction
- String matching and prefix-based search
- Computational limits and approximation
- Integrative algorithms project
