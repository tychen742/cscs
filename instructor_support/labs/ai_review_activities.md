# Integrated AI Review Activities

All three activities work without AI access. The candidates are prepared teaching examples, not attributed transcripts from a named model. Students first derive the contract and expected outcomes independently; an instructor may permit a live assistant for explanation or extra test proposals.

Canonical answers are the new hide-input code cells in the corresponding content notebooks. Their outputs are retained. New semantic exercise IDs are cscs-methods-ai-packing-review, cscs-testing-ai-counterexamples, and cscs-text-ai-import-review. Existing assignment and exercise IDs are unchanged.

For grading or discussion, ask the student to explain the original defect, identify a counterexample, show the observed results, and adapt the code to one changed requirement. If permitted AI help was used, record the tool/date, advice accepted or rejected, revision, and independent checks. A pasted conversation or passing only AI-proposed ordinary cases is insufficient evidence of understanding.

Use these activities in place of comparable practice rather than adding a required teaching week. Keep the course policy explicit. The optional post-OOP AI application extension is a separate application-design track with recorded-response demonstrations; no live provider or paid account is required for these review activities.
